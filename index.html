Humanise Design

Design that doesn’t just respond — it feels like it belongs there.

You can tell when something has been designed around you, rather than simply placed in front of you.

That’s what I’m interested in now — designing for presence, movement, and perception, not just what someone sees on a screen.

From VR and AR to spatial audio and motion, every project is another attempt to make digital experiences feel a little more human.
